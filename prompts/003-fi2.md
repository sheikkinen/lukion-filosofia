## Expected answer, written before the work [M]

1. **Ten topics** (order 11–20): what morality is (morality, law, custom); relativism and objectivism; is and ought;
   virtue ethics; consequentialism; duty ethics and Kant; rights and human rights; the good life (Epicurus, the
   Stoics); relationships and life choices; animals, nature and climate.
2. **Primary texts** in Greek (Aristotle, Plato, Epicurus, Epictetus), Latin (Seneca), English (Hume, Bentham, Mill,
   Locke, Wollstonecraft) and German (Kant), all public domain, with open editions. Some English originals
   (Bentham, Mill) are on Gutenberg or Wikisource; the Akademie edition of Kant is on the Internet Archive or
   Wikisource.
3. **Sensitive themes:** euthanasia, suicide and abortion may appear in applied ethics. FI2 treats them as cases with
   arguments on both sides, a content note, a help box and no methods, or leaves them to TE3-style care. Animal and
   climate ethics must avoid moralising.
4. **The exam** asks FI2 questions in every sitting (ethical theories compared, an applied case, the good life), so
   each topic carries one exam task in the filosofia formats.
5. **Images** within about $0.70: ten nano-banana scenes, a few Mermaid diagrams (the three normative theories, is and
   ought, the moral circle), and public-domain portraits (Kant, Mill, Bentham, Wollstonecraft) from Commons.

## The work

1. **Research** (`scout.js`), five researchers: the FI2 curriculum and how it builds on FI1; the primary texts per
   topic with opened original-language editions and passage references; materials to link; the exam's FI2 tasks
   (2023–2026) and their formats; sensitive themes and worldview neutrality in ethics. The critic proposes the topic
   list with codes and passages, the link inventory, the image plan and any contract additions.
2. **Content** (`module-content.js`, module FI2): a data agent (FI2 into `subject.yaml`, codes fetched, materials,
   facts) and the lead writer (FI2 topic 01, style-guide additions) in parallel; three writers; four checkers
   (curriculum, sources and translations, worldview and neutrality, pedagogy); one fix round; the judge.
3. **Images:** an art director, one tool runner, the renderer, PD artworks with Commons records; checks; the judge.
4. The producer commits, pushes (the site is public, D-005) and checks the live pages.

## Limits

At most $1 for images. Nothing copied from publishers, YTL, Loeb pages or modern copyrighted translations.
