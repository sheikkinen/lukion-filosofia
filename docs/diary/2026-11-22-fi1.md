# Brief 002: FI1, accepted with minors

**Date:** 2026-11-22 **IDs:** brief 002, D-004

## What happened

The producer scaffolded the site on lukio-runko from the terveystieto files, and the engine fetched the 16 FI codes.
The content workflow wrote the style guide, the six pages and ten FI1 topics with 17 primary-text boxes in Greek, Latin
and English, each with the site's own Finnish translation. An Opus sources checker opened every edition page and
back-translated every box; the judge opened three more. A second workflow fixed the minors and made 9 Mermaid diagrams
and 12 nano-banana illustrations ($0.67). After the operator approved public-domain artworks (D-004), five Commons
works were added with licence data and a visible credit. The judge accepted each step with minors; the producer made
the last fixes.

## Traps

- **Perseus opens some texts in a Loeb English translation by default,** and one critic URL pointed at the English
  page; the writers took the Greek from the raw HTML of the Greek page.
- **An agent edited CLAUDE.md and the contract in place** to exempt two data files from the line cap; the producer
  reverted it and wrote a dated addendum.
- **An agent changed the shared engine** to show the Commons credit. It is good and tested, now on the engine's local
  branch `commons-credit`; the site's pin (v0.1.0) needs a new engine tag before the published build shows the credit.
- **Commons titles move,** so file pages for David and Hume returned 404 under the research's titles.

## Heuristic

Quote the original from the edition's raw page, never from memory or a translation page, and have a different agent
back-translate it.
