# Brief 001: filosofia research

**Date:** 2026-11-22 **IDs:** brief 001, D-001 to D-003

## What happened

Seven researchers and a critic read the curriculum (FI1–FI4 from ePerusteet), the public-domain primary texts, Finnish
translations and the quotation right, the materials to link, the exam, images, and the terveystieto site as the base.
The critic proposed ten FI1 topics covering every FI1 code, each with a primary-text passage in Greek, Latin or
English, and an image plan of 12 nano-banana scenes, 9 Mermaid diagrams and 5 public-domain artworks for about $0.60.
The producer wrote the report and the content contract.

## Traps

- **Perseus defaults to Loeb English** on several texts; only the Greek may be quoted.
- **Gutenberg was down all day,** so no public-domain Finnish translation could be verified; own translations are the
  default.
- **The filosofia exam has no A/B/C parts,** and its deduction rule differs from terveystieto's.

## Heuristic

Quote the original beside the translation: it is public domain, and it lets a reader check the translation.

**Seed:** A sources-and-translations checker that back-translates every quote into English could become a shared tool
for any subject that quotes old texts.
