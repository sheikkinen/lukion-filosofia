# 002: FI1 *Johdatus filosofiseen ajatteluun*: the site on the engine, ten topics with own translations, and images

**Written before the work.** Producer's brief, 2026-11-22. The producer scaffolds the site; then two workflows:
`module-content.js` (adapted for philosophy) for the topics, and an images workflow.

## Why

The operator [O]: "Start FI1", in answer to "Start the FI1 content brief (topics, translations and images)?" after the
research (brief 001, `docs/research/2026-11-22-research.md`).

**Serves:** G-1, G-3. **Goal type:** technical (checks) with look. **Chain:** brief 2. **Models:** the data agent,
writers, checkers and image renderer on Sonnet; the lead writer, the translation checker and the judge on Opus; the
paid image tool run by one named agent.

## Expected result, written before the work [M]

1. **The site builds on `lukio-runko` v0.1.0** with FI1 in `subject.yaml`, codes fetched (FI.yt 6; FI1 5/5), six
   pages in Finnish, the rubric section on the curriculum page (osuvuus, eheys, vakuuttavuus), and a Pages workflow
   with `TZ: Europe/Helsinki` ready for when the operator publishes.
2. **Ten FI1 topics** from the critic's list, every FI1 code covered by a topic and an exam task. Each topic has one
   to three primary-text boxes: the public-domain original and the site's own translation, checked by a second agent
   who opened the edition page. The exam tasks use the filosofia formats of the contract (§B): definitions, concept
   and material essays, multiple choice, method tasks and 30-point tasks in parts.
3. **Images within about $1:** 12 nano-banana scenes, about 9 Mermaid diagrams and up to 5 public-domain artworks
   from Wikimedia Commons, with their source and credit. Each picture is named in its page's text.
4. **Likely traps:** links that fail (Gutenberg, some Perseus Greek pages), the engine's date scanner flagging "1.7"
   citations and its link pattern breaking on parentheses, Opiskelijalle at the 600-word limit once boxes are added.

## The work

1. **Scaffold (producer):** `subject.yaml`, `requirements.txt`, `.github/workflows/pages.yml` (with TZ), the diagram
   tools from terveystieto (`tools/render_diagrams.py`, `tools/mermaid-config.json`, `tools/package.json`), and the six
   pages as drafts for the lead writer.
2. **Content (`module-content.js`, module FI1):** a data agent (codes fetched, `data/materials.yaml`, the six pages'
   facts) and the lead writer (topic 01, the style guide, the six pages, the rubric section) in parallel; three
   writers for 02–10; four checkers: curriculum, sources and translations (opens every edition page, checks each
   passage at its reference, back-translates), worldview and neutrality, pedagogy and exam tasks; one fix round; the
   judge.
3. **Images:** an art director (Mermaid sources, nano-banana prompts, PD artworks with licence data); one tool runner;
   a renderer; image and browse checkers; one redo round; the judge.
4. The producer commits locally. Publishing (a public repository and Pages) waits for the operator.

## Limits

At most $1 for images in this brief. Nothing copied from publishers, YTL, Loeb pages or modern copyrighted
translations. The repository stays local.

## Done when

The operator can open the built site locally and read ten FI1 topics, each with a primary text in the original and in
Finnish, exam-style tasks with model answers, linked materials, and pictures. Internally: every FI1 code covered, every
translation checked against its original, the checks passed or named, and the judge accepts.
