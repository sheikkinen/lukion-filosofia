## Exam shape (all seven sittings) [V]
Nine tasks, 120 p. Part 1: six tasks of 20 p, answer 3-5. Part 2: three tasks of 30 p, answer 0-2. Five tasks in
all. Grading dimensions: osuvuus, eheys, vakuuttavuus. Top-mark answers "deepen" with other theories or the
tradition. Typical formats: short definitions (4x5 p, 600 or 750 characters), 20-item multiple choice or
matching, 20 p essay (often with material), 30 p tasks split 15+15, 10+20, 5+5+20 or 5+5+10+10.

## What the exam asks, by theme
- Morality vs law and custom: K24 t4, S24 t4, S25 t2, K26 t4. Four sittings; a fixed short-answer format, 4x5 p.
- Relativism and descriptive vs normative: K24 t7, K25 t4, K26 t2, K26 t7, K23 t9 (weak). Both essay and 750-char parts.
- Is and ought: Hume's guillotine as a 4 p definition (S25 t2); fact, moral claim and opinion (K24 t7).
- Normative theories compared or applied: S23 t2, S23 t4, K24 t2, K25 t2, S24 t8, S25 t4, S25 t9, K26 t9. Seven of seven
  sittings have at least one. Cases used: trolley, noble lie, Gandhi, abortion, plagiarism, family vs climate work.
- Good life and meaning: S23 t8, S24 t2, K25 t7 (Epictetus). Three sittings; Stoicism twice, Epicureanism listed.
- Animals and nature: K23 t2, K23 t4, K24 t8. Climate appears only as a case in K23 t8 and S25 t4. No pure climate task.
- Rights: K23 t5, S25 t8, S24 t8, K26 t5. Mostly credited to FI3; K23 t5 is rights vs duties.
- No task so far on virtue ethics alone, on euthanasia, or on Kant's text; every ethics task allows any theory.

## What each FI2 topic must carry for exam coverage

Common to all ten: one exam-style task in an exam format, a note on how top answers deepen (second theory,
tradition source). Never reuse past YTL tasks.

1. moraali-laki-ja-tapa: four marks of morality (bindingness, independence, universality, overridingness); a
   2x2 grid of acts against morality, law, custom; moral agent and subject. Formats: `maarittele-ja-esimerkki`
   (4x5, 600 chars) and one example-giving task per combination.
2. onko-ja-pitaisi: Hume's guillotine as a definition; fact vs moral claim vs opinion; why mixing them hurts public
   debate. Formats: `maarittele-ja-esimerkki`, a 750-char sort-the-claims part, one 15 p essay.
3. objektivismi-relativismi-subjektivismi: descriptive vs normative relativism, cultural relativism, subjectivism,
   the problems (self-refuting, no basis for criticism, tolerance as a universal norm), metaethical vs normative.
   Formats: 20 p `kasite-essee` and an `aineisto-essee` with a short video-like or text material.
4. hyve-etiikka: the virtuous agent as the measure, flourishing, vices, and the plagiarism, prejudice, truth-telling
   cases. Format: `kahden-teorian-vertailu` plus a 5 p "why wrong by this theory" part.
5. seurausetiikka: utilitarianism, act or omission, trolley, what to maximise, objections (rights, demandingness).
   Formats: trolley-style `ajatuskoe-arviointi` and `kahden-teorian-vertailu`.
6. Kant, duties and rights: categorical imperative as a 4 p definition, duty vs inclination, means and end, rights
   and duties as correlates (K23 t5). Formats: `maarittele-ja-esimerkki`, `kahden-teorian-vertailu`.
7. hyvä elämä ja merkitys: Epicurus, the Stoics (Epictetus), experience machine, existentialist meaning,
   assessment of current use of Stoicism. Format: 30 p in parts, 15 p explain with text plus 15 p assess;
   a 20 p essay on a life philosophy.
8. ihmissuhteet ja elämänvalinnat: a dilemma with competing duties and values (care for family vs social goals),
   no single right answer, apply one or more theories and compare their limits (particularism, relational ethics). Format:
   `ajatuskoe-arviointi` with material, 20 p. Keep personal-disclosure prompts out.
9. eläimet, luonto ja ilmasto: value of nature (instrumental, intrinsic, anthropocentrism, biocentrism,
   sentientism), moral status, Regan, Singer, Nussbaum, speciesism, animal use in research, climate as justice
   and responsibility. Formats: statement-matching (10x2), a 30 p task with an author text and a second-theory
   part. Climate needs added emphasis since the exam has used it only as a case.
10. ihmis- ja perusoikeudet: human, basic and civil rights and their link, natural rights, rule of law, rights vs
    duties, abortion as a rights case (sensitive: both-sides, help box). Formats: `aineisto-essee` with 2-3
    materials, a 750-char pair for and against, a 15+15 split.
